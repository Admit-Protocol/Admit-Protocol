# The name

"Admit Protocol" names this specification and the project that maintains it. The specification text is free to copy and adapt under CC BY 4.0, and the schemas and examples under MIT. The name is not part of either license.

You may:

- say that your server or agent **implements Admit Protocol**, or **implements Admit Protocol 0.1**, when it conforms to [CONFORMANCE.md](CONFORMANCE.md);
- say that your school **accepts applications through Admit Protocol** when it serves a conforming implementation;
- refer to the specification, link to it, quote it, and write about it.

You may not:

- use the name, or a name confusingly close to it, for a product, service or specification that does not conform, especially one that scores or ranks applicants or that lets an AI write for the student;
- use the name in a way that suggests the project endorses or operates your implementation, or that yours is the official one;
- register the name or a close variant as a trademark, domain or account name for a related product.

A fork of the specification that changes the three rules in section 2 must use a different name.
